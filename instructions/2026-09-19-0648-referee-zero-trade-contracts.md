## Your job

The six checks your definition sets out, in order, each with its result written
down: count · independence · grounding · the outcome and its split in numbers ·
the reasoned objection (RULES 32) · scope.

Two things about this particular jury that you must judge rather than assume:

- **The jurors reported leaks in the question they were given.** Read their
  reports and read the question yourself. Decide what those leaks do to the
  independence check. You decide this; nobody has decided it for you.
- **The jurors were told they may not set a threshold** (RULES 33). At least one
  of them raised, itself, whether its own answer is a definition or a threshold.
  That is a scope question and it is yours.

**You do not answer the question.** If you judge all three jurors wrong, you
refuse and say why. You never write what you think the right answer is.

Begin with a single line that is exactly `RATIFIED` or `REFUSED`, then the six
checks with their results, then what follows from your verdict.
