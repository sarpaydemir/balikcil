### The recipe as the exam candidate receives it

> 2. Check the blockers first. If any of these is true, write "no trade" and stop
>    scoring: the `taker L/S` column is needed for your answer; any hour prints
>    `open int` = 0; a `depth` column repeats one identical value for three or
>    more consecutive hours; the funding payment count does not match the stated
>    interval; the quantity you want comes from the After section.
> 3. Read down the 24 rows of the Before table and look at the hourly change
>    column. If the card prints `chg%`, use it. If it does not, compute
>    close(h)/close(h-1) - 1 for each row.
> 4. **If any one of those 24 values is 5.00% or more in size, in either
>    direction, the movement score is 1. Otherwise it is 0.**
> 5. If the movement score is 1: answer that a large movement is coming. **You
>    have no direction.** If the answer form makes you pick up or down, pick one
>    and write confidence **0**, and say in the ledger that the recipe supplied
>    no direction.
> 6. If the movement score is 0: **you have not been told the moment is calm.**
>    You have been told the recipe cannot see. Answer from a 50/50 prior between
>    "a large movement" and "stays calm", and write a low confidence.
> 7. Fill in the ledger for the card: score (0 or 1), the raising signal (the
>    magnitude gate, fired or not), the blockers you checked, and the unknowns —
>    which on every card include: announcements (never fetched), direction (the
