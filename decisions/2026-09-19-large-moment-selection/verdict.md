## Verdict

**RATIFIED — 2–1**

The section prescribes that the 48-hour separation constraint ("Of two moments closer than 48 hours to each other, only the larger counts") operates **during the selection process**, not after it. Moments are taken largest-first; each new moment must be separated by at least 48 hours from every moment already taken; selection stops when the quota is reached. This reading is supported by two independent jurors who ground it in the clause that the count "shrinks in proportion to its lifetime: one moment per 18 days" — a statement about yield that is only true if the separation constraint is satisfied while the quota is being filled.

**Split: 2–1 in favor of prune-during-selection.**
