One row per open question. This index carries no wording, no options and no
numbers of any question; the content is only in the file named in each row.
Rows that name each other in the third column go to the same jurors, so each
of those rows lists every file its group needs. "Files a juror needs" is the
whole reading list: a juror is given those files and nothing else, and the
canteen book only by the line ranges named. RULES 33–35 apply to every row.
A row whose status is "withdrawn" is not to be commissioned; its file says
why. A row whose status is "ratified" is answered; its verdict is named.

## The order in which the groups sit

- **Now:** the JQ-N1 group with JQ-CANTEEN-8 (being answered).
- **Before the DATE/CONTENT group:** the JQ-R04-CARRIES group (JQ-R04-CARRIES-a
  and JQ-R04-CARRIES-b). It may sit at once. The DATE/CONTENT group is not
  commissioned until it is ratified.
- **After the JQ-R04-CARRIES group is ratified:** the DATE/CONTENT group
  (JQ-R04-DATE-a, -b, -c and JQ-R04-CONTENT-a, -b, -c), given the
  ratification sentence of the JQ-R04-CARRIES verdict and nothing else of it.
- **At any time:** JQ-R04-CONTENT-d, alone.
- **The three R-04 groups still open have three separate sets of jurors.**
  No juror of the JQ-R04-CARRIES group, of the DATE/CONTENT group, or of
  JQ-R04-CONTENT-d sits on either of the other two, and none of them is given
  another group's file or answers (beyond the one ratification sentence
  named above).
