Derived numbers used repeatedly, and how they were computed from the card:
- **before-24h move** = close at h-1 divided by close at h-24, minus 1. Computed
  by me from the two closes printed on the card.
- **after move** = the card's own "Measured 24-hour move" line (read, not computed).
- **hot hours** = how many of the 24 before-hours have |chg%| >= 2.00, counted
  off the chg% column.
- **book/turnover** = (depth -1% + depth +1%) at h-1, divided by the "avg hourly
  volume" on the Previous-7-days line. Computed by me.

## Part 3 — ideas (all three parts, or it is not an idea)

I1 · **Trigger:** the before-24h move (close h-1 over close h-24) is at least +1.5%, and at least one of the last three before-hours printed a chg% of +2.00 or more. **Direction:** sell (short) at the first price after the start hour. **Exit:** flat at the close 24 hours after entry, or out early if price makes a new high above the h-1 close. **Where it appears in this batch:** exactly 7 cards match — C025, C063, C153, C171, C261, C281 (all six are large moments and all six fell, between -10.4% and -26.3%) and C203 (calm, +1.94%). Five different coins (BCH, FARTCOIN, KOMA x2, NOK, ZRO) and six different months, so it is not one coin's quirk; but C025 (2025-11-18) and C063 (2025-11-20) fall in the same week and both ran with a falling BTC, so they may be one event under RULES 13. 7 cards is an observation, not a rule · 3

I2 · **Trigger:** the before-24h move is -3% or worse. **Direction:** buy at the first price after the start hour. **Exit:** flat at the close 24 hours after entry. **Where it appears in this batch:** 11 cards — large: C285 (+22.2), C100 (+104.0), C048 (+13.2), C273 (+16.0), C117 (+34.7), five for five up; calm: C130 (+2.1) and C075 (+4.1) up, C119 (-4.0), C287 (-3.5), C102 (-4.0), C042 (-0.2) down. So 7 of 11 up, and all the large ones up. The win comes from the large cards only, and nothing on the "before" side of a card tells you which kind you are holding · 3

I3 · **Trigger:** the previous-7-day high-low range printed on the card is above 50%. **Direction:** no direction — this is a "something will move" filter only, to be combined with a directional rule; on its own I would not trade it. **Exit:** n/a for a filter; if forced, 24 hours. **Where it appears in this batch:** 6 cards, C100, C153, C273, C171, C117, C105, all 6 large. But they are only 3 coins (FHE x3, KOMA x2, ZRO), and 11 of the 17 large cards do not trigger it, so it is narrow and possibly a property of those coins rather than of the measure · 2

## Part 4 — what I looked at and did not find, and what I could not read

N1 · Before saying "no missing card": all 34 cards listed in `data/card-order/v2/batches/batch-01.md` were opened and every one had a complete `## Before` table of 24 rows, a complete `## After` table of 24 rows, a "Moment kind" line and a "Measured 24-hour move" line. No card was missing, truncated or unreadable. No technical failure to report.
N2 · Where I looked for the depth data: the two `depth -1%` and `depth +1%` columns on every card. Not one of the 34 cards had an empty or `.` depth cell in either section. Four cards carry depth values I would not trust, and I have noted each: C287 (three identical consecutive after-hours), C041 (three identical consecutive before-hours), C053 (one 2.49M after-hour against a 700k baseline), C042 (3.51M/3.54M at +2/+3 against a 1.8-2.0M baseline).
N3 · **Measurement limit on trade count:** above 1000 the trades column is rounded to whole thousands ("1k", "2k", "3k"). Any average-ticket-size measure (volume divided by trades) is therefore coarse to within a factor of about 1.5 for the many hours that print "1k", and I did not compute one. Below 1000 the column prints exact figures (e.g. 754, 388, 185).
N4 · **Measurement limit on 24-hour volume averages:** C158's h-24 alone is about 52x its weekly average hourly volume, which dominates any 24-hour average for that card. Where I used a volume ratio I used the h-1 hour against the 7-day average rather than a 24-hour mean, precisely so that one hour cannot carry the number.
N5 · Fields I deliberately did not read, because they are not my field of view: taker buy%, open interest, L/S acct, top L/S pos, taker L/S, funding, exchange announcements, Wikipedia page views, prediction market, US releases. I make no statement of any kind about them, including whether they were present.
