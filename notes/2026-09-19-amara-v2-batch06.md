# Amara · watcher-high · round 1 · batch 06 · field of view: the outside world

Field of view: Binance and Korean exchange announcements · the US release calendar ·
Wikipedia page views · the prediction market · the state of bitcoin and ethereum over
those hours. Nothing outside this field is noted here.

**How I measured, so the numbers can be checked.** Where I write "BTC 24 h" or "ETH 24 h"
I mean the arithmetic sum of the 24 hourly BTC / ETH `chg%` figures printed in that card's
**after** table. It is my own arithmetic on the card's own numbers (an approximation of the
compounded move, slightly off for large moves); it is not a figure printed on the card and
nothing comes from outside the card. "Kind", "measured 24-hour move", release names, view
counts and the Polymarket / announcement wording are quoted from the card.

**Batch counts.** Every count below is out of the **34 cards of batch 06 only**. It is a
floor, not a count over the 306. Batch 06 holds **19 large** cards (C151 C195 C110 C263
C187 C027 C166 C034 C060 C019 C067 C017 C056 C109 C213 C194 C043 C226 C247) and **15 calm**
cards (C052 C165 C092 C051 C121 C055 C089 C073 C146 C134 C050 C199 C087 C197 C118).
