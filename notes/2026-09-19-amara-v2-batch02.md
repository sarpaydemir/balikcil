## G · Ideas (trigger · direction · exit)

G1 · **Idea — market-drop carry-through (weak; constructed after reading the cards, so it carries the "afterwards" label of RULES 6).**
- **Trigger:** the additive sum of BTC's last 6 hourly % changes is ≤ −3.0%.
- **Direction:** sell (short) the coin.
- **Exit:** 18 hours after entry, or earlier if BTC's cumulative change measured from the trigger hour returns to 0.0%, whichever comes first.
- Grounding: C282, C296, C046, C049 — in each, BTC fell ≥5% over the after-window and the coin fell 1.1×–2.8× as much in the same direction (C1). **Rests on 4 cards out of 34, and those 4 are at most 3 distinct market days (C296 and C046 are the same day, D5) — so it is really 3 events.**
- **Objection I raise against my own idea:** this is market beta restated. A plain price-momentum rule on the coin itself would likely fire at the same moments, so it may add nothing over the "last 24 hours continue" rival in RULES 11. It must be tested *against* that rival, not against a coin flip.
- Confidence: 2

G2 · I have **no second idea** from this field. The announcement field is a fetch failure (A1), the prediction-market field is empty on all 34 cards (A5), Wikipedia exists on 5 cards and is lookahead-contaminated (E2), the release calendar does not separate the two kinds (B1) and its only striking entry rests on 2 distinct events with opposite outcomes (B2, B3), and the pre-move BTC/ETH state does not separate the two kinds (C7). Writing a trigger/direction/exit on any of those would be inventing one. · 5
