(my definition requires this), never as a signal of their own. I did not look at
taker buy%, open interest, long/short ratios, funding, announcements, Wikipedia or
prediction markets — those are somebody else's field.

all 34 cards; no card had an empty depth column. The `trades` column is present on all

## Ideas (trigger · direction · exit) — all three parts, or it is not an idea

**Idea L1 — blow-off reversal.**
Trigger: the close 1 hour before the decision hour is more than +20% above the close 24 hours earlier, **and** the last two hourly volumes are each at least 10x the 7-day average hourly volume.
Direction: **sell** (short).
Exit: close at the fixed horizon of 24 hours after entry, or earlier on a stop at +10% against the entry.
Rests on: C139 (trigger +53.8%, ~23x and ~37x volume; outcome -39.29%) and C107 (trigger +23.0%, ~1.4x and ~2.3x volume by the 7-day measure but +33.57%/+14.78% price; outcome -52.36%). **2 of 34 cards.** C107 only half-satisfies the volume half of the trigger, so the volume clause may have to be dropped or the threshold moved. This is an observation dressed as an idea; it cannot be evidence from two cards, and the price half of it is visible to anyone looking at the chart, so it may add nothing over the price itself.

**Idea L2 — book thins on both sides.**
Trigger: at the decision hour both `depth -1%` and `depth +1%` are at their lowest value of the preceding 24 hours.
Direction: **buy**.
Exit: close 24 hours after entry; no intermediate exit, because in 12 of the 21 large cards the biggest hour lands in the last eight (section F).
Rests on: C251 (+37.91%) and C191 (+30.09%). **2 of 34 cards.** Against it: C131 has the `+1%` column at its window minimum and is calm; and six cards in this batch carry depth values I do not trust (section H), so the field this idea is built on is the weakest field on the card. Confidence that this survives a blind test: low.

**Idea L3 — do not exit early.**
Trigger: any long or short entry taken at a card's start hour under any other rule.
Direction: inherits the entry's direction.
Exit: hold the full 24 hours rather than closing at 6 or 12 hours.
Rests on: the count in section F — in 12 of 21 large cards (C249 C251 C196 C289 C201 C306 C272 C276 C112 C033 C191 C180) the largest single hour falls at +16..+23, and only C059 and C139 have theirs at +0..+3. This is an exit-design observation, not a signal; it costs nothing to test and it changes what every other rule measures.
