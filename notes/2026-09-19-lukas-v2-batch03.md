## H · Ideas (trigger · direction · exit) — untested, and I say what they rest on

**Idea L1 — "loud last six hours".** Rests on **2 cards of 34** (C227, C254), with two knife-edge counterexamples (C113, C250).
- **Trigger:** at the start hour, over the preceding 6 hours, mean hourly |chg%| ≥ 2.0% **and** mean hourly quote volume ≥ 2.0x the coin's previous-7-day average hourly volume.
- **Direction:** buy if the sum of those 6 hourly chg% is positive; sell if negative.
- **Exit:** close 24 hours after entry, or on a 5% adverse move from the entry price, whichever comes first.
- Honest note: at a 1.5x volume threshold the rule picks up C113 and C250 and gets both directions **wrong**; at the stated 2.0x it also misses C125 (+85.61%) and C207 (-11.44%), which it would have called right. It should be tested at several thresholds, and it will fail if the two supporting cards are the whole of it.

**Idea L2 — "quiet coin, no position".** A blocker rather than a trade, rests on **3 cards of 34** (C137, C144, C149).
- **Trigger:** over the 24 before-hours, mean hourly |chg%| < 0.5% **and** mean hourly volume < 1.2x the 7-day average.
- **Direction:** no position in either direction (blocker; it removes cards, it does not open them).
- **Exit:** the block lifts 24 hours after the trigger hour.
- Honest note: this is proposed **only** because C144/C149/C137 show that such windows are unreadable — C149 went +104.21% from exactly this state. It is a claim about ignorance, and its worth is whether it removes more wrong answers than right ones. If a scored recipe would otherwise guess on these cards, this says do not.

I have **no third idea**. The depth-collapse reading (C099) has a trigger and a direction but I will not write it as an idea, because it rests on one card whose numbers I believe may be broken (section G).
