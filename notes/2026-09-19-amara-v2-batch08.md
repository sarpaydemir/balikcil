## D · Ideas (trigger · direction · exit), and one non-idea

D1 · C068 C045 C255 C071 C242 C297 C192 C133 C030 C126 · **Idea — release-hour majors follow-through.** *Trigger:* the card's After window contains a CPI, PPI or Employment Situation print at hour H, and in hour H or H+1 bitcoin's printed hourly change exceeds +1.0% (long leg) or −1.0% (short leg). *Direction:* trade the coin in the same direction as that bitcoin hour. *Exit:* close at the end of hour H+4, or immediately if bitcoin's hourly change reverses past 1.0% the other way. · Built from C9; C10 is the standing counterexample set. I am writing this as an idea only because it has all three parts — on my 34 cards it has 6 supporting instances and 4 failures, which is not evidence of anything. · 2

D2 · C225 C133 C238 C071 C028 · **Idea — fade the majors-only day.** *Trigger:* in the last 6 hours of the Before window bitcoin's summed hourly change exceeds +2.0% while the coin's own summed change is under +0.5% (the coin ignored a market rally). *Direction:* sell the coin. *Exit:* 24 hours, or on a 3% adverse move. · Motivated by C133 (BTC +5.75% across the window, coin −2.17%) and C238 (BTC +5.5% in two hours, coin +2.6%). I did not test the trigger on all 34 cards — I am naming the condition so somebody can. Low confidence on purpose. · 1

D3 · not an idea, an observation · **I could not build a size (large-vs-calm) trigger out of my field.** Announcements are dead on all 34 cards (A1); the prediction market is empty on all 34 (A5); Wikipedia exists on 5 cards of one coin and its usable, pre-window part shows nothing (C11); the release calendar splits 8/17 large vs 7/17 calm in the after window (A8) and 8/17 vs 5/17 in the before window (A7); and both the size and the direction of bitcoin's before-window move fail (C3, C4). The overlapping pair C028/C106 (C1) shows why: two coins, one identical bitcoin/ethereum background, one calm and one +120.84%. · Writing this as an observation rather than an idea because it has no trigger, no direction and no exit — per the rule, it does not count as an idea and must not be smuggled in as one. · 5
