## 5 · Ideas (three parts each) — both weak, both stated so they can be tested and killed

**Idea A — amplified market-drop continuation.**
- *Trigger:* an hour `h` in which BTC's hourly change ≤ -1.5% **and** the coin's same-hour change is at least 3× BTC's in the same direction.
- *Direction:* **sell** (short) at the open of hour `h+1`.
- *Exit:* close 6 hours after entry, or immediately on the first BTC hour ≥ +1.0%, whichever comes first.
- *What I actually saw:* the ≥3× filter fires on **4 cards in this batch** — C234 (+1: BTC -1.92, coin -7.83 → next hour -5.05, continued), C099 (+22: BTC -2.06, coin -13.24 → next hour -35.29, continued), C178 (+8: BTC -0.74/ETH -2.47, coin -2.87 → next hour -0.73, continued), C113 (-10: BTC -0.76/ETH -1.73, coin -7.46 → next hour **+1.49, reversed**). 3 of 4 continued. **Four cards is an observation, not a rule**, and two of the four (C099, C178) are themselves market-wide events. Without the 3× filter, over the 9 instances I could find (C300, C234, C099, C178, C207, C254, C189, C144, C113) it is roughly a coin flip: 5 continued, 4 did not.
- *Confidence: 2.*

**Idea B — market-wide up-burst follow-through.**
- *Trigger:* an hour in which BTC ≥ +1.5% **and** ETH ≥ +1.5%.
- *Direction:* **buy** at the open of the next hour.
- *Exit:* 6 hours after entry, or a -2% stop from entry, whichever comes first.
- *What I actually saw:* ~7 instances in this batch (C299 +6, C189 +9 and +10, C094 +3, C300 +9, C280 -22, C248 -9): roughly 3 small gains, 2 losses, 2 flat over the following hours. **In this batch it looks like a coin flip.** Note it also fires on calm cards (C189, C300, C248) as readily as on large ones — which is exactly the failure mode my definition warns about.
- *Confidence: 2.*

**Not an idea — a blocker, stated as one (RULES 31).** When any other rule fires, the size and sign of BTC/ETH over the same hours should be recorded next to the result, and any case where the market moved with the coin at comparable size (C099, C207, C178, C094, C299 in this batch) should be flagged as a market event rather than counted as a coin finding. This has no trigger/direction/exit and I am **not** calling it an idea.
