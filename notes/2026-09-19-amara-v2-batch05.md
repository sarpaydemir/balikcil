## How I measured BTC/ETH (so the numbers can be checked)

"BTC24" and "ETH24" below mean: **the arithmetic sum of the 24 hourly `BTC` (or
`ETH`) percentage values printed in the card's own table for that window.** It is
my own addition of numbers printed on the card, not a compounded return, and it
is not printed anywhere on the card. I round to 0.1 pp. Where I call a sum an
estimate I say so. Anything I state that is not on a card (e.g. the usual clock
time of an FOMC statement) is marked as my opinion.
