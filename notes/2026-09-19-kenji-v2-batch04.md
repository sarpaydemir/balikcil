## Ideas (trigger · direction · exit)

**Idea K-04-1 — two-cohort divergence.**
- **Trigger:** at the start hour, `L/S acct` at h-1 is at least 10% below its h-24
  value **and** `top L/S pos` at h-1 is at least 8% above its h-24 value, **and**
  every before-window funding payment is within ±0.002% of +0.0050%.
- **Direction:** buy.
- **Exit:** close 24 hours after entry, or earlier if `L/S acct` rises back above
  its h-24 value.
- Fired in 3 of the 34 cards of batch 04 (C251, C059, C179); all 3 large, all 3
  up; 0 of the 13 calm cards fired it. **Three cards is not evidence** — it needs
  the blind exam, and the count over the whole set is not mine to make.

**Idea K-04-2 — the anti-signal.**
- **Trigger:** at the start hour, a before-window funding payment of +0.02% or
  more has occurred, with open interest within ±5% over the same 24 hours.
- **Direction:** sell (i.e. do not take a long; if the recipe would otherwise
  score a buy, this is a blocker).
- **Exit:** the condition lapses when the next payment falls back under +0.02%.
- Fired in 5 of the 34 cards (C112, C103, C163, C131, C108); 4 were calm. I offer
  this as a **blocker candidate**, not a trade, because a signal that mostly
  marks "nothing will happen" earns its keep by stopping trades, not by opening
  them.

I have **no third idea.** The OI-build and top-L/S-pos observations above lack a
direction, so under RULES 8 they stay observations.
