## Ideas (trigger · direction · exit)

Only one candidate in my field has all three parts. I state it as a candidate to
be tested, not as a finding — it rests on 2 cards out of 34.

**Idea K-1 · top-trader long/short collapse**
- **Trigger:** at the start hour, `top L/S pos` at h-1 is ≤ 0.80 × its value at h-24 (a fall of 20% or more in the top-trader long/short position ratio across the preceding 24 hours), measured on the coin's own series.
- **Direction:** buy.
- **Exit:** close 24 hours after entry, unconditionally.
- **Evidence in batch 07:** fires on 2 of 34 cards (C223, C066). Both are large moments, both up (+23.86%, +32.37%). It fires on 0 of the 21 calm cards; the deepest fall among calm cards is C200 at -10%. Recall is 2 of 13 large cards.
- **Against it:** n = 2. The milder 5–15% version of the same measurement splits 4 large / 4 calm (C128 C136 C170 C193 / C037 C015 C186 C200), which is what a threshold fitted to two points usually looks like. C223 is NIL and C066 is FARTCOIN, so at least it is not one coin.

Everything else in my field I write as an observation, not an idea, because it
lacks a direction: elevated funding (C152 C136 C170 C157 C260) marked 5 large
moments but split 2 up / 3 down, and is confounded by KOMA.
