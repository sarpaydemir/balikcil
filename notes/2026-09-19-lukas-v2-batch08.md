## C · Ideas (trigger · direction · exit)

**Idea L-1 — mean reversion after a very deep week.**
- **Trigger:** the card's previous-7-day price change is -35% or worse.
- **Direction:** buy.
- **Exit:** close 24 hours after entry; hard stop at -10% from entry.
- Rests on **3 cards** in my batch: C115 (7d -41.81% → +46.39%), C284 (7d -37.58% → +17.09%), C126 (7d -46.47% → -6.79%). Two of three up. C297 (7d -22.35% → -1.25%) sits just outside the trigger and would have been a flat loser had the threshold been -20%.
- Confidence **2**. Three cards is an observation, not a rule, and the threshold was chosen after looking at the numbers, which makes it an "afterwards" rule under RULES 6.

**Idea L-2 — volatility-expansion filter as a gate, not a trade.**
- **Trigger:** at least one hourly close-to-close change of |5%| or more in the 24 hours before the decision hour.
- **Direction:** none on its own — it fired 5 down and 3 up across C169, C068, C115, C229, C150, C168, C114, C106. It can only be used as a **gate** in front of some other directional signal, or as the "is this a large moment at all?" half of the exam's first question.
- **Exit:** 24 hours after entry (see B8 — a +12h exit would truncate two thirds of the large cards).
- Rests on **8 cards** fired, **0 false fires among 17 calm cards**.
- Confidence **3** for the gating use, **1** for any directional use.
- Blocker I would attach: do not use it where the triggering hour is itself inside an ongoing move (C168, C106, C114, C169) until the exam's hidden-price cards show it still fires.

**Idea L-3 — the exit discipline, as a rule about exits rather than entries.**
- **Trigger:** any position opened on any signal in this laboratory.
- **Direction:** unchanged from whatever opened it.
- **Exit:** hold the full 24 hours; do not exit at the mid-point and do not stop-and-reverse inside the window.
- Rests on **all 34 cards**: less than half the move was in by +11 in 11 of 17 large cards, and the sign at +11 matched the final sign in 17 of 17 large and 14 of 17 calm.
- Confidence **3**. It is a costing and exit-design point rather than a source of edge, and it is measured from the after section, which the exam will not show.

Things I looked at and could **not** turn into an idea, because I could find no direction: order book depth level, depth trend, bid/ask depth asymmetry, pre-start volume ratio, prior-7-day trend agreement with the prior 24 hours. All are written up as negative results in B5, B6 and B4.
