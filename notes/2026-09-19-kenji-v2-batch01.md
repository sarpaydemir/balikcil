## Ideas (trigger · direction · exit)

**Idea K1 — crowd adds into a rally.**
- **Trigger:** at the moment's start hour, both hold over the preceding 24 h: (a) close at h-1 is ≥ +5% above close at h-24, and (b) open interest at h-1 is ≥ +2% above open interest at h-24.
- **Direction:** sell (short).
- **Exit:** flat 24 hours after entry, or on a +6% adverse move, whichever comes first.
- **Cards it fires on in batch 01:** C025 (-10.44%), C153 (-21.00%), C063 (-26.31%), C261 (-13.33%), C105 (**+50.56%**, wrong way). 4 of 5 in the intended direction. It fires on 0 of my 17 calm cards.
- **Coins:** BCH, KOMA, FARTCOIN, NOK, FHE — 5 distinct coins, which is the one thing I like about it.
- **What weakens it:** 5 cards is 5 cards, and it is an idea, not evidence. C025 and C063 carry a large market-wide component (see the RULES 13 notes), so two of the four successes may be one market event rather than two coin events. The nearest calm card, C041 (+5.31% price), misses only because its OI is +1.94%, a hair under the 2% line — the separation is not clean, it is borderline.
- **Confidence: 3.**

**Idea K2 — an hour of book growth says "something is coming", not which way.**
- **Trigger:** at least one hour inside the preceding 24 h in which open interest rose ≥2% on the previous hour.
- **Direction:** this trigger gives **none** — in batch 01 it fired on 9 large cards, 4 up and 5 down. So it can only be used as a size/volatility filter that another rule supplies direction to, never on its own. Written here as a half-idea so the canteen can attach a direction to it or throw it out.
- **Exit:** n/a until a direction exists.
- **Counts in batch 01:** 9 of 17 large, 3 of 17 calm (C130, C212, C075).
- **Confidence: 3 as a size filter, 1 as anything directional.**

I have no third idea. Everything else I saw in my field is written above as an observation because it lacked a direction, an exit, or both.
