# Kenji · watcher-high · field of view: the crowd · round 1 · batch 01

Field of view: open interest (OI), long/short account ratio (`L/S acct`), the
ratio of large players (`top L/S pos`), taker buy/sell pressure (`taker buy%`
and `taker L/S`), and the funding rate itself.

Cards read: 34 of 34 in batch 01. Batch composition, counted by me from the
"Moment kind" line: 17 `large`, 17 `calm`.

Conventions used below, so that every number is traceable to the card:
- **"OI before-change"** = (open interest at h-1) / (open interest at h-24) - 1,
  both values read off the card's Before table. My arithmetic.
- **"L/S acct max"** = the largest of the 24 hourly `L/S acct` values in the
  Before table.
- Any mean I quote is the arithmetic mean of the 24 hourly values on that card,
  computed by me. Where I did not actually sum the column I write "estimate".
- I read only my own columns. Price, volume, trade count, depth, announcements,
  Wikipedia and prediction-market fields were on the cards but are not mine;
  I refer to price only where my definition requires it (to check whether a
  signal merely restates price, and to check whether BTC/ETH moved too).

## C · Ideas (trigger · direction · exit) — candidates to be tested, not findings

I1 · **Trigger:** at the moment's start hour, open interest at h-1 is more than 7.5% above open interest at h-24, *and* the coin's close at h-1 is within ±3% of its close at h-24 (the price-flat form). **Direction:** this trigger in my batch flagged size but not sign — of the 6 cards over the OI line, 4 fell and 2 rose, so as a directional rule I can only write the price-flat subset, which was C004 and C120, both **sell**. **Exit:** flat 24 hours after entry, or on the first hourly close back below the entry price minus 0 (i.e. a fixed 24-hour hold), whichever comes first. **Seen in:** 6 of 34 cards for the size form (C139, C004, C120, C106, C270, C221), 2 of 34 for the directional price-flat form (C004, C120) · opinion: two cards is an anecdote; I write it as an idea only because it is testable · 2

## D · Data quality and things I could not read

D1 · **`taker L/S` contains broken cells.** Measured extreme values: C143 After +1 hour = **2981.81**; C009 h-8 = 30.92 and h-11 = 24.87; C098 h-24 = 33.26 and After +4 = 9.72; C221 h-15 = 18.45; C218 After +12 = 19.01 and +16 = 17.38; C143 h-4 = 23.56 and h-10 = 20.63; C192 h-11 = 13.29. All fall in hours with low trade counts. A rule keyed to `taker L/S` would be keyed partly to these · 5

D2 · **`top L/S pos` contains one-hour level shifts far too large for the accompanying price move.** Measured: C019 After +17, 4.51→1.87 (that hour's price change −0.04%); C023 After +18, 1.10→1.58 (−0.71%); C153 After +21, 2.03→1.51 (−3.22%); C110 before h-10, 1.55→2.28 (−2.66%); C104 before h-17, 1.06→1.32 (+0.71%); C255 After +0, 3.05→2.20 (−0.86%); C009 After +2, 2.13→2.93 (+0.80%); C182 After +0, 1.21→1.06 (−1.03%). Eight such breaks across 34 cards · opinion: I read these as discontinuities in the source series, which means any rule measuring the *change* in this column will partly be measuring the breaks · 4

D3 · **`L/S acct` shows single-hour spikes/dips that revert immediately**, e.g. C271 h-17 = 1.46 between 1.65 and 1.64; C271 h-9 = 1.45 between 1.66 and 1.60; C270 h-16 = 1.32 between 1.66 and 1.45; C221 After +16 = 1.08 after 1.58. Same caution as D2 · 3

D4 · **Funding resolution is coarse.** The card gives only the 3 or 6 payment values per window, not an hourly rate, so I cannot see funding move within an hour or measure a rate of change. I looked at the "Funding:" line of all 34 cards; it was present and populated on all 34. No card in batch 01 had "interval changed: yes" — I checked that field on all 34.

D5 · **Open interest is quoted in different units across cards** — contracts/tokens for most coins but what looks like a much smaller unit for C004 and C009 (AVGOUSDT: 12.33k, 18.50k) and for the BCH cards (269k–385k). I used percentage change only, which is unit-free, and I did not compare OI levels across coins.

D6 · **Nothing in my field was missing on any of the 34 cards.** Where I say "none" or "flat" above, I read the actual column. The `MISSING` markers on these cards are all on fields outside my field of view (Wikipedia page views, exchange announcements, prediction market) and I did not use them.

D7 · **No failures to report.** All 34 files named in `data/card-order/batches/batch-01.md` opened and parsed; none was truncated, empty or unreadable.

D8 · **Batch-composition caveat for every count above.** These counts are out of 34 cards only, and they are floors, not counts over the 306-card set. Coin concentration in batch 01 is heavy: FHEUSDT appears on 9 cards, KOMAUSDT on 5, NILUSDT on 5, BCHUSDT on 4, ZROUSDT on 4, NEWTUSDT on 3, AVGOUSDT on 2, FARTCOINUSDT on 1, NOKUSDT on 1. Every cross-card count above is therefore also partly a count of coins.
