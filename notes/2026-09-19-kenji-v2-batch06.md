## Part 3 · Ideas (trigger · direction · exit)

Each of these is an idea, not evidence. The card counts are out of 34.

**Idea K1 — elevated funding on a flat price**
- Trigger: on a coin with a 4 h funding interval, at least one of the six
  before-window funding payments is >= +0.050%, AND the 24-hour price change
  over the before window is between -5% and +5%.
- Direction: buy, at the first price after the start hour.
- Exit: close 24 hours after entry; hard stop at -8%.
- Seen in: C151, C166, C110 — 3 of 34 cards, 2 distinct coins, all three large
  up moves (+41.49%, +17.55%, +65.60%). No card in the batch fired this trigger
  before a down or calm moment.
- Against it: 3 cards is nothing; KOMA supplies two of them; and I cannot rule
  out that high funding simply marks the coins that were already hot that week
  (C151's previous 7 days were +21.72%, C110's +18.84%; C166's were +0.00%,
  which argues the other way).

**Idea K2 — the account crowd leaving the long side**
- Trigger: `L/S acct` at h-1 is at least 4% below `L/S acct` at h-24.
- Direction: sell, at the first price after the start hour.
- Exit: close 24 hours after entry; hard stop at +8%.
- Seen in: fires on 7 of 34 cards — C195, C034, C060, C194, C247 (all large
  down), C073, C121 (calm). Never fired before a large up move in this batch.
- Against it: in all five of the down cases BTC and ETH fell in the after window
  as well, so by RULES 13 these may not be five independent events. Two of the
  seven firings paid nothing. And in C195 the price also fell across the before
  window, so for that card the trigger is partly a restatement of price.

**Idea K3 — a big rally that open interest did not pay for**
- Trigger: price rose >= 10% over the 24 before-hours AND open interest at h-1
  is at least 2% below open interest at h-24.
- Direction: sell, at the first price after the start hour.
- Exit: close 24 hours after entry; hard stop at +8%.
- Seen in: C060 (+10.6% price / -4.2% OI, then -19.65%) and C134 (+11.8% /
  -2.6%, then -8.39%) — 2 of 34 cards.
- Against it: two cards is an observation, not a rule. The 10% floor was chosen
  after looking at C087 (+4.4% / -3.6%, calm) and C121 (+2.5% / -2.9%, calm),
  which is fitting to what I saw and must be labelled as such. The trigger also
  needs price, which is not my field, so half of it is a restatement of price.
