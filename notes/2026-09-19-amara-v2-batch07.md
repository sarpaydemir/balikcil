## D · Ideas (trigger · direction · exit)

Direction: none of its own — **suppress** any coin-specific interpretation and treat the move as one market event (RULES 13). Exit: n/a. D4 · **No idea can be offered from the US release calendar.** A release before the moment marks large moments at 43% against a 38% base rate (C6), a release inside the window at 38% (C7), and the one apparent timing effect has a plain clock-of-day explanation (C9). I could construct a trigger, but every direction I could attach to it is unsupported by the 34 cards I read.
