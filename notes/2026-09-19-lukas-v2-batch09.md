# Lukas · watcher-high · v2 batch 09 · round 1

Field of view: **price, volume, trade count, order book depth, volatility.**
Cards read: 34 (all of batch 09). Round 1 — I read no other watcher's notes.

All counts below are **out of the 34 cards of batch 09 only**. They are floors,
not counts over the whole set.

Definitions I used, so the numbers can be checked:
- **before-move** = close at h-24 → close at h-1, in per cent.
- **before-range** = (highest before-hour close − lowest before-hour close) ÷ lowest, in per cent.
- **after-move** = the card's own stated "Measured 24-hour move".
- **7d avg vol** = the card's own "avg hourly volume" line for the previous 7 days.
- Volume sums and means I computed by adding the card's own hourly `quote vol` column; these are rounded.

Format: `card no · what I saw · why I think so · how sure I am (1–5)`

## Part 3 — ideas (trigger · direction · exit)

**Idea L1 — buy a deep, jagged fall.**
- Trigger: the close-to-close move over the previous 24 h is ≤ −4% AND the high-low range of the last 24 hourly closes is ≥ 8%.
- Direction: **buy**, at the first price after the start hour.
- Exit: close 24 h after entry; cut earlier if price falls 8% below entry.
- Batch-09 evidence: fired on 6 of 34 cards — C032 (+17.61), C140 (+41.47), C270 (+20.94), C062 (+22.54), C232 (+24.44), C142 (+1.50). No card met the trigger and fell. 6 cards, no costs applied, and C142 shows the trigger also picks up calm moments.

**Idea L2 — short a last-hour volume blow-off.**
- Trigger: the last before-hour's quote volume is ≥ 3× the previous 7 days' average hourly volume.
- Direction: **sell (short)**, at the first price after the start hour.
- Exit: close 24 h after entry; cut earlier if price rises 10% above entry.
- Batch-09 evidence: fired on 4 of 34 cards — C078 (−27.65), C120 (−44.40), C221 (−38.18), C004 (−17.27). All four fell; highest ratio on any calm card was C074 at 1.95×, below the line. 4 cards only. C004 is a tokenised stock whose volume follows US equity hours, so one of the four may be a clock artefact; and C221's fall was market-wide.

**Idea L3 — fade the last 24 hours instead of following it.**
- Trigger: |close-to-close move over the previous 24 h| > 4%.
- Direction: **opposite** to that move (buy if it fell, sell if it rose).
- Exit: close 24 h after entry; cut earlier at 10% against entry.
- Batch-09 evidence: fired on 12 of 34 cards; 8 winners, total +144.1 points of raw 24-hour move, mean +12.0 per trade before costs. The buy half (7 trades, +116.6, 6 winners) carried it; the sell half (5 trades, +27.5, 2 winners) rests on C078 and C221 and contains a −35.10 loss on C054. I would only put the buy half forward.
