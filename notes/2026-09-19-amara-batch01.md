## E · Ideas (trigger · direction · exit) — one only, and its in-batch record is a coin flip

**Idea 1 (weak; recorded so it can be killed by script rather than by argument).**
- **Trigger:** the card's release calendar shows a CPI, PPI or Employment Situation release at hour +k of the after window, and the BTC hourly change printed at hour +k or +k+1 exceeds ±1.0%.
- **Direction:** trade the coin in the same direction as that BTC hour.
- **Exit:** close 6 hours after entry, or at the end of the 24-hour window, whichever comes first.
- **In-batch record:** for on 3 cards (C255 NOK -4.52% with BTC -2.00% and it kept falling; C120 FHE -6.72% with BTC -1.19%; C033 BCH +1.97% with BTC +1.39% and it kept rising), against on 3 cards (C143 KOMA +1.09% with BTC +1.82% and the card closed -2.09%; C217 NIL -0.32% with BTC +1.00%; C192 NEWT +0.48% with BTC +1.14% and then flat). 3 for, 3 against out of 6 — that is a coin flip in this batch and I would not expect it to survive RULES 11.
- Entry must respect RULES 16: the release hour is known in advance, so the fill cannot be that candle's open.

I have no second idea. Everything else my field produced is a null, and a null written as an idea would be dishonest.

## F · What I could not do

- I could not evaluate **announcements** at all on any of the 34 cards. The source failed; per RULES 21 that is a technical failure, not the finding "announcements do not matter". Nothing in this batch bears on whether a Binance or Korean listing/delisting/warning precedes a large movement.
- I could not evaluate **Wikipedia** on 30 of 34 cards, and the 4 I could are one coin at daily resolution. Nothing here bears on whether attention precedes a movement for coins the title rule failed on.
- I could not evaluate the **prediction market** on any card: the source was reached and is genuinely empty in these hours on all 34.
- My BTC/ETH 24-hour figures are arithmetic sums of hourly percentages done by hand, not compounded returns. They are good to roughly a tenth of a percent for the purpose I used them (comparing magnitudes), and anybody building a rule on them should recompute them by script.
