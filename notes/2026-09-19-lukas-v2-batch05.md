"before-vol" = sum of the absolute values of the 24 `chg%` entries in the Before table
(a realized-volatility proxy; it is arithmetic on the card's own numbers, not an estimate).

## Ideas (each has trigger, direction, exit; none of these is evidence)

I01 · **extreme last-hour volume**. Trigger: at the start hour, the immediately preceding hour's `quote vol` is >= 8x the median of the 23 hours before it AND that hour's `chg%` is positive. Direction: buy. Exit: close at the 24th hour after entry, or immediately on a -10% adverse move from entry, whichever comes first. Support in batch 05: **1 card of 34** - C104 (11.2x, h-1 +2.49%, outcome +161.78%). No other card in the batch reaches 8x; the next highest ratios are C164 5.3x (calm), C173 5.3x (large, and the move was DOWN), C084 4.7x (calm). Opinion: at 8x this is a one-card observation and at any looser threshold it stops separating (see X06). I write it because it is the only thing in my field with a testable shape, not because I believe it · 2

I02 · **drained last hours after a 24-hour decline**. Trigger: at the start hour, the before window's net move (h-24 close to h-1 close) is <= -3% AND the mean `quote vol` of the last 4 hours is <= 0.6x the before-window median. Direction: buy. Exit: close at the 24th hour after entry, or on a -8% adverse move from entry, whichever comes first. Support in batch 05: C252 (before -13.4%, last-4h volume ratio about 0.35, outcome +24.24%) and C156 (before -3.9%, last-4h ratio about 0.55, outcome +27.86%); C160 (before -3.0%) is borderline on the volume leg and gave +4.26%. Against: C202 (before -3.3%, but last-4h volume ratio about 1.0, so it does not trigger) went -13.60%. **n = 2 clean triggers out of 34 cards.** Opinion: two cards is an observation, not a rule; I give it a shape only so it can be counted properly later · 2
