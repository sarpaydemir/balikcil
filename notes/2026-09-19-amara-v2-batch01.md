## How I measured the BTC/ETH numbers

The `BTC` and `ETH` columns are hourly percent changes. Where I write a 24-hour
figure I mean **the arithmetic sum of the 24 hourly values** in that section
(before or after), rounded to 0.1. It is an approximation of the compounded
move, not the compounded move itself — I mark it "sum" everywhere. Everything
else is copied off the card.

## G · Ideas (trigger · direction · exit)

**Idea 1 — Wikipedia attention on a coin that has an article.**
Trigger: the card's Wikipedia field is present, and (last full day before the
start) ÷ (mean of the 7 days before) ≥ 1.05. Direction: **sell**. Exit: the
close 24 h after the start hour.
Cards it fires on in this batch: C025 (ratio 1.26, outcome -10.44%) and C044
(1.08, -14.36%) — **2 cards, both of one coin, both correct**. It does not fire
on any of the 5 calm BCH cards (0.81–0.93) and it misses C048 (0.75, +13.16%).
Two events is an observation, not a rule; and the field exists on only 8 of the
34 cards I read, so even if it worked it would be silent on 76% of cards.
Confidence: 2.

**Idea 2 — market co-movement as a blocker, not an entry.**
Trigger: some other rule has produced a buy or sell signal on a card, **and**
the BTC 24-hour sum over the before window has the same sign with |value| ≥ 3%.
Direction: **no trade** — a blocker in the sense of RULES 31, not a direction.
Exit: not applicable, the rule refuses entry rather than opening one.
Grounds: on the 8 cards where |BTC after sum| ≥ 3% (C285, C025, C048, C063,
C281, C044, C077, C273) the coin's move is a market move amplified 2–5×, so a
coin-level "finding" measured on those cards is counting one event several
times. I state it as a blocker precisely because the before-window BTC sum does
**not** predict anything (section D, means 1.76 vs 1.73) — it is a bookkeeping
guard, not an edge. Confidence: 3.

I have **no third idea**. Announcements, the prediction market and the release
calendar produced nothing with all three parts, so they are written as
observations above, not as ideas.
