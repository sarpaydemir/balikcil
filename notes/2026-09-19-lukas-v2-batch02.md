## How I measured (so the numbers can be rechecked)

Two quantities are my own arithmetic on columns printed on the card; everything
else is read straight off the card.

- **BV ("before volatility")** = the sum of the 24 `chg%` values of the before
  section, taken as absolute values. Units: percentage points per 24 h.
- **Vol-ratio** = (largest hourly `quote vol` in before-hours h-6..h-1) ÷ (my
  eyeballed median hourly `quote vol` of the 24 before-hours). The denominator
  is an **estimate** read off the column, not a computed median.
- **Depth-trend** = mean `depth -1%` over h-6..h-1 ÷ mean over h-24..h-19.
- "before net" = close at h-1 ÷ close at h-24 − 1.

Batch-02 reference values, for reading the per-card lines:
BV — large-moment cards: median 21.3, range 9.1 (C088) to 69.9 (C116), n=15.
BV — calm cards: median 17.5, range 4.7 (C016) to 32.7 (C224), n=19.

## Ideas (trigger · direction · exit)

**Idea 1 — buy a volume-climax down-flush.**
Trigger: in the four hours ending at the decision hour, some hour's `quote vol`
is ≥ 5x the median hourly `quote vol` of the preceding 24 h **and** that hour's
`chg%` is ≤ −2 %.
Direction: buy.
Exit: close 24 h after entry; hard stop at −8 %.
Batch-02 evidence: fires on C283 (11.6x, −7.46 % hour, after +43.11 %) and C018
(3.1x — would need the threshold at 3, not 5 — after +10.06 %). Against it:
C116 fires at 5.5x on a −5.34 % hour at h-3 and the after is **−69.08 %**, which
alone would wipe out both wins. C022 does not fire (2.9x) and was calm, which is
the right answer. So 2 wins, 1 very large loss, in 34 cards. Confidence 2.

**Idea 2 — continue a strong before-trend.**
Trigger: before-window net price change ≥ +10 % over the 24 h.
Direction: buy.
Exit: close 24 h after entry.
Batch-02 evidence: fires on exactly two of the 34 cards — C244 (+16.1 % before,
+32.47 % after, win) and C116 (+32.8 % before, −69.08 % after, loss). One for
one, and the loss is four times the size of the win. Confidence 1. I write it
only because it is testable and because someone will otherwise propose it.

I have **no** three-part idea from the depth or trade-count columns. What I have
there are the two null results above (depth-trend does not separate; trade count
is redundant to volume), and those are observations, not ideas.

## Data defects and things I could not measure

Not results — failures (RULES 21). One by one:

1. **C018** — `depth -1%` is the constant value `3.19k` in all 48 rows (24 before
   + 24 after) while `depth +1%` runs 3.87M–6.03M. The other four BCH cards show
   bid depth of 0.42M–4.67M (C016, C022, C046, C049). The value is implausible
   and frozen. I could measure no bid-side depth on this card.
2. **C183** — `depth -1%` reads `140.79` (no `k` suffix) for seven consecutive
   after-hours, +3 through +9, against 48k–96k in the before section and 68k–87k
   from +10 onward, while `depth +1%` over the same hours is 122k–220k. A ~1000x
   one-sided drop for exactly seven hours. Unusable for those hours.
3. **C235** — `depth -1%` reads 409k, 415k, 391k, 431k in after-hours +5..+8
   against 24k–41k on both sides of that block, `depth +1%` unchanged at 35–46k.
   Suspect.
4. **C291** — `depth -1%` reads 1.66M and 1.67M at before-hours h-12 and h-11
   against 170k–270k elsewhere, `depth +1%` unchanged at ~180k. Suspect; same
   shape as item 3.
5. **C016** — `depth +1%` moves 15.84M → 8.25M → 14.30M → 2.60M → 1.91M → 2.07M
   within single hours while `depth -1%` holds near 3.1–3.8M. Either the ask book
   genuinely vanished and returned repeatedly, or the column is unreliable. I did
   not use C016's ask depth.
6. **C135** — the `trades` column reads exactly `3k` for all 24 before-hours
   while `quote vol` ranges 6.99k–63.98k; the after section of the same card
   mixes raw counts and rounded ones (464, 4k, 613, 4k, ...). The before-section
   trade count is unusable.
7. **Trade-count column, batch-wide** — counts below ~1000 are printed exactly
   (e.g. 813, 480, 646) and counts above are rounded to one significant figure
   (`1k`, `2k`, `27k`). So trade count has ~±50 % granularity in the middle of
   its range. Every trade-count statement I made above inherits that.
8. **Order-book depth is the median resting notional within 1 % of mid**, per the
   card header. It says nothing about depth further out or about how fast the
   book refilled. I could not measure book resilience on any of the 34 cards.
9. **Nothing was missing at the card level.** All 34 files named in
   `data/card-order/v2/batches/batch-02.md` opened and all carried a complete
   24-row before table and a 24-row after table. No card was absent or truncated.
