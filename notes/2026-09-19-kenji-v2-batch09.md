## Ideas (trigger · direction · exit)

C007,C004,C078,C221,C120,C140,C270 · **Idea 1 — open-interest build.** Trigger: at the candidate hour, open interest at h-1 is ≥ +5% above its value at h-24 while the coin's h-24 -> h-1 price change is inside ±10%. Direction: sell (short). Exit: close 24 h after entry, or earlier on a close 5% against the entry. Basis: of the 7 large cards in this batch meeting the OI part, 5 fell (C007, C004, C078, C221, C120) and 2 rose (C140, C270); no calm card in this batch meets it. The ±10% price filter is there to keep C078 (price +26% in the window) from being a price rule in disguise · 2

C288,C020,C032,C286 · **Idea 2 — negative funding with a still book.** Trigger: at least one funding payment in the 24 h before is negative AND |OI at h-1 vs h-24| < 3%. Direction: buy. Exit: close 24 h after entry. Basis: C288 (+14.64%), C020 (+11.00%), C032 (+17.61%) all satisfy it and all rose; C286 satisfies it too (all six payments negative, OI -1.0%) and fell 7.40% as a calm moment. 3 of 4 in one batch — I write it because it is testable, not because I believe it · 2

C120,C005,C142,C216 · **Idea 3 — large traders buying the last dip.** Trigger: the top-trader position ratio at h-1 is ≥ +15% above its lowest value inside the before window AND the last three hourly closes are lower than the close three hours earlier. Direction: sell (short). Exit: close 24 h after entry. Basis: C120 (+37% off its window low, -44.40%) and C005 (+17% off its low, -9.70%). The two calm cards with the biggest top-trader rises, C142 (+12%) and C216 (+13%), fall below the 15% line — which is why the line is at 15%, and that is exactly the kind of after-the-fact fitting that makes this a 2 and not more · 2

- No separation from **funding at the floor** (5 large, 6 calm) or from **elevated positive funding** (4 large, 7 calm, 5 of the 7 one coin).
