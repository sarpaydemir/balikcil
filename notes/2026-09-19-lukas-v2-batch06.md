# Lukas · watcher-high · field of view: price itself (price, volume, trade count, order book depth, volatility)

Round 1 · batch 06 (34 cards, positions 171–204) · written 2026-09-19.
All counts below are **out of the 34 cards of batch 06 only**. They are floors, not counts over the set.

Two derived numbers are used repeatedly. Both are arithmetic on numbers printed on the card:

- **q12** = the largest single-hour |chg%| among the last 12 hours of the "before" section (h-12…h-1).
  A measure of how flat price was going into the start hour.
- **vmax×** = (largest single-hour `quote vol` in the "before" section) ÷ (the card's stated
  "previous 7 days · avg hourly volume"). A measure of how big the biggest hour of the last day was
  against the week's normal hour.

`b24%` = close at h-24 → close at h-1, computed from the two printed closes.

Direction: **not supplied by this trigger** — of the nine cards it fires on, 4 went up and 5 went down. Exit: 24 hours after entry. In batch 06 this is right 5 times (C263, C043, C060, C017, C187), wrong 3 times (C213, C027, C034) and undefined once (C166, 7-day change +0.00%). 5 out of 8 is not a finding and I expect Viktor to kill it; it is written out so it can be measured rather than argued about · 2
