## H · One idea, with all three parts - and its counter-examples named

H38 · C094 C243 C254 C207 C125 C090 C246 C001 · **candidate idea** (from C12):
- **Trigger:** at the start hour, the 24-hour change in open interest (h-24 -> h-1) is >= +8% or <= -8%, **and** the coin's own close over that same window is up.
- **Direction:** buy.
- **Exit:** flat at the close 24 hours after entry, unconditionally (I have no basis for a longer horizon - the cards stop there).
Applied to batch 03 this fires on 6 cards: C094 (+23.81%), C243 (+140.96%), C254 (+29.19%), C125 (+85.61%), **C207 (-11.44%)**, **C246 (-3.12%)**. It does not fire on C090 or C001 (price leg negative).
· opinion: 4 winners, 2 losers on 6 events; the total is carried almost entirely by one card (C243 +141%), and the price leg of the trigger is a restatement of price, so the only new information is the OI leg. **Six events across six coins in one batch is an observation, not evidence** - it is written in three parts only so that somebody can actually shoot it down in the blind exam and the money test · 2
