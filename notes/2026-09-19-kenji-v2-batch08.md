Field of view: open interest (OI), long/short account ratio (`L/S acct`), ratio of
large players (`top L/S pos`), taker buy/sell pressure (`taker buy%`, `taker L/S`),
the funding rate itself.

Cards read: 34 — C210, C225, C169, C271, C068, C126, C205, C228, C101, C071, C115,

open interest at h-1 divided by open interest at h-24, minus 1. "Baseline funding"
= +0.0050% per payment, which is the value printed on the great majority of the
4 h-interval payments in this batch.
